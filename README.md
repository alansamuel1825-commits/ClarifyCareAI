# ClarifyCare AI

### Understand. Decide. Act.

ClarifyCare AI is an AI-powered Support Navigation Platform that transforms confusing documents, notices, forms, and life situations into clear explanations, personalized action plans, and trusted support resources.

Built for the USAII Global AI Hackathon 2026.

---

## The Problem

Every day, students, parents, caregivers, and community members receive important information they struggle to understand.

School notices, support eligibility documents, healthcare instructions, housing forms, government letters, and community service information are often written using complex language and scattered across multiple sources.

People miss deadlines, lose opportunities, and fail to access available support—not because help does not exist, but because understanding it is difficult.

During stressful situations, people need more than information.

They need clarity.

---

## Our Solution

ClarifyCare AI converts complex information into actionable guidance.

Users can upload documents, images, PDFs, notices, forms, or simply describe their situation using text or voice.

The platform analyzes the information and provides:

- Plain-language explanations
- Key information extraction
- Deadline identification
- Urgency assessment
- Personalized action plans
- Resource recommendations
- Multilingual assistance
- Accessibility support
- Progress tracking
- Confidence indicators

Our goal is to help users move from confusion to action.

Instead of simply generating responses, ClarifyCare:

- Extracts deadlines
- Identifies urgency
- Builds action plans
- Recommends resources
- Tracks progress
- Supports accessibility
- Provides multilingual guidance

The goal is not conversation.

The goal is meaningful action.

## Responsible AI

### Realistic Risk

AI may occasionally misunderstand a document or provide incomplete guidance.

### Mitigation

ClarifyCare provides:

- Confidence indicators
- Source transparency
- Human review recommendations
- Safety-focused design

### Human-in-the-Loop

ClarifyCare never makes:

- Legal decisions
- Medical decisions
- Financial decisions
- Educational decisions

Humans remain responsible for final decisions.
